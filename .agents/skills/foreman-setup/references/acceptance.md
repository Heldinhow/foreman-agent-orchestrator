# Setup skill acceptance

Tracked in [issue #6](https://github.com/Heldinhow/foreman-agent-orchestrator/issues/6). This specification covers an instruction skill, not an executable init command.

## Scenarios

1. **Fresh workspace:** the Router confirms memory/privacy and source scope before research, then gathers product goals and users, not just tooling preferences.
2. **Multiple sources:** target and reference repositories remain distinct; independent Workers return revision-backed findings and typed, directional dependencies.
3. **Unknown relationship:** unsupported or contradictory evidence remains a hypothesis/gap and becomes an interview question instead of an invented edge.
4. **Interview routing:** a Worker asks the Router; the Router supplies sourced known context or asks the human under the existing grill procedure.
5. **Interruption:** a fresh Router finds answered topics, pending questions and Worker assignments in setup/status records and resumes without duplicate research.
6. **Confidential workspace:** private findings use a confirmed protected store; public records contain only safe references. Ignored records are not claimed to clone across machines.
7. **Update:** new repository evidence is merged into owning records and invalidated assumptions reopen gaps; existing decisions are not silently overwritten.
8. **Readiness:** only operator-confirmed scope without blocking gaps is ready. Partial setup preserves its limitations and next actions.
9. **Roadmap:** a requested proposal uses outcomes, constraints and dependencies; missing business priorities are asked, not inferred from code.
10. **Host/tool limitation:** unavailable research tools or access produce explicit blockers, not a Router subagent, guessed findings or elevated permissions.

11. **Seven-service catalog:** all seven operator-provided repositories appear with stable identities, responsibilities, project-note links and verified or unresolved T3 mappings. No service disappears because it was inaccessible.
12. **Cold restart:** a new Router without the original conversation recovers the product, every repository summary, dependency graph, current decisions and ongoing work from persisted records; missing history or private access is explicit.
13. **Material discussion capture:** a corrected responsibility, rejected alternative, research finding or unresolved question updates its owning record and history before a later assignment relies on it; a failed write leaves dependent work blocked.

## Authoring and structural checks

Apply AI Hero `writing-for-agents` and its skill mechanics: intentional model invocation, branch-specific pointers, one home per fact/procedure and observable completion criteria. Source: https://www.aihero.dev/skills-writing-for-agents .

Check frontmatter, matching directory/name, reference paths, root discovery pointers and `git diff --check`. Review scenarios against the written paths, preserving safety and behavior rather than optimizing solely for word count.

Static checks and scenario walkthroughs are not live onboarding validation. Actual discovery, independent Worker research, interview resumption and multi-repository memory retrieval require separately recorded live evidence.
